How to play

  • Whoever holds the ball throws it. Get hit and you're out. Last kid standing wins.
  • Holding the ball you can only shuffle. Hold it three seconds and the ref takes it.
  • The field shrinks as kids go out.
  • Score: your place, two per hit, three per catch.
