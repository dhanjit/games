**Page body**

Twenty kids on a grassy field and one tennis ball. Whoever holds it throws it at anyone. Get hit and you're out. Catch it and it's yours. The ref voids throws from too close ("no air gap") and takes the ball if you hold it too long. The field shrinks as kids go out. Last one standing.

Distance is the whole skill. From five yards there's no time to dodge, from fifteen it's a duel, from twenty-five the ball drops first. Slide under a throw, brace to catch one, and pick your moment.

Made for the school game some of us grew up with. Runs in the browser, installs as an app, works on a phone held sideways.

**Controls** — WASD run · mouse aim, click throw · hold click or C to brace and catch · space to slide · Esc pauses. Touch: left thumb runs, tap right to throw, hold right to catch, swipe to slide.
