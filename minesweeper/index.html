Sweep

Minesweeper with no edges. The field goes on forever in every direction; your score is how much of it you reveal. One mine ends the run.

  • Tap / click — reveal a cell
  • Tap a number — with its mines flagged, opens the rest around it
  • Right-click / long-press — flag a mine
  • Drag or WASD / arrows — pan
  • Wheel / pinch / + − — zoom
  • ◎ / O — glide back to where you started
  • R — new field · H — this help

Numbers count mines in the eight neighbours, as ever. Far away is just more field — click anywhere.
